50/30/20 BUDGETING RULE

The 50/30/20 rule is a simple budgeting guideline.
Allocate 50% of your income to needs (rent, groceries, bills),
30% to wants (entertainment, dining out, shopping),
and 20% to savings and debt repayment.

EMERGENCY FUND

An emergency fund should cover 3 to 6 months of living expenses.
Keep it in a separate savings account that is easily accessible.
Start small by saving a fixed amount each month and gradually increase it.

TRACKING EXPENSES

Track every expense, no matter how small.
Categorize your spending into groups like food, transport, bills,
entertainment, shopping, health, and education.
Review your spending weekly to identify patterns and areas to cut back.

REDUCING FOOD EXPENSES

Cook meals at home instead of eating out.
Plan your meals for the week and buy groceries in bulk.
Avoid impulse purchases by making a shopping list before going to the store.
Carry homemade lunch to work or school to save on daily food costs.

SAVING ON TRANSPORT

Use public transport instead of cabs or personal vehicles when possible.
Carpool with colleagues or friends for daily commutes.
Walk or cycle for short distances to save money and improve health.

MANAGING SUBSCRIPTIONS

Review all your subscriptions monthly.
Cancel services you no longer use or rarely use.
Look for free alternatives or bundle deals to reduce costs.
Avoid signing up for free trials that auto-convert to paid plans.

AVOIDING IMPULSE SPENDING

Wait 24 hours before making any non-essential purchase.
Unsubscribe from marketing emails and notifications.
Set a monthly fun money budget and stick to it.
Use cash instead of cards for discretionary spending to feel the impact.

DEBT MANAGEMENT

Pay off high-interest debt first (avalanche method).
Alternatively, pay off smallest debts first for motivation (snowball method).
Avoid taking new debt while paying off existing loans.
Always pay more than the minimum due on credit cards.

SETTING FINANCIAL GOALS

Set specific, measurable financial goals with deadlines.
Break large goals into smaller monthly targets.
Examples: save 50000 in 6 months, reduce food spending by 20% this month.
Review and adjust your goals quarterly.

INVESTING BASICS

Start investing early to benefit from compound interest.
Diversify your investments across different asset classes.
Do not invest money you may need in the short term.
Consider low-cost index funds for long-term wealth building.
Consult a financial advisor before making large investment decisions.

BILL MANAGEMENT

Set up automatic payments for recurring bills to avoid late fees.
Review your bills monthly for any errors or unexpected charges.
Negotiate with service providers for better rates periodically.
Keep a calendar of due dates to stay on top of payments.

SMART SHOPPING

Compare prices across multiple stores before purchasing.
Use cashback apps and loyalty programs when shopping.
Buy generic or store-brand products instead of name brands.
Shop during sales and discount events for big-ticket items.
Avoid buying items just because they are on sale.

BUILDING GOOD FINANCIAL HABITS

Automate your savings by setting up recurring transfers.
Review your finances at the end of each month.
Celebrate small financial wins to stay motivated.
Educate yourself about personal finance through books and trusted resources.
